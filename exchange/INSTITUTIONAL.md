# BirrEX 0.9.0 — Institutional Edition (pre-authorization)
## Scope
Educational portfolio, ledger, P2P and consumer-protection examples; informational USD market dashboard; owner-only Cloudflare Access workspace with aggregate operational status. No real custody, execution, deposits, payments, escrow, customer onboarding or settlement. The trial balance is a fixed fictional accounting example, not a financial ledger implementation or financial statement.

## Risk register
Unauthorized financial activity: keep execution absent and real balances disabled. Identity and data protection: minimize collection, owner-only access, verified claims, retention and privacy review. Operational security: review dependency inventory, CSP, key rotation, incident response and recovery tests. Consumer protection: risk disclosures, complaint escalation and independent review. Financial integrity: independent accounting specification, reconciliation and audit before any financial product. Market data: provider rights, quota monitoring, timestamps and stale-state handling.
