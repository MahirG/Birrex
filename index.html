03 / TRUST BY DESIGN

Security is not
a decoration.

The proposed engineering approach puts verifiable safeguards first. These are design requirements, not claims of deployed controls or certification.

PROPOSED CONTROLS
01

Identity & access

Multi-factor authentication, session protection and identity verification.

02

Data protection

Encryption at rest and in transit, least-privilege access and retention controls.

03

Transaction integrity

Atomic ledger design, reconciliation, audit trails and independent security assessment.

04

Regulatory readiness

Applicable authorizations, legal review and verified reporting before any operational launch.

04 / THE VISION

Local understanding.
Global design standards.

BirrEX explores what a transparent, accessible Ethiopian market interface could look like. This independent concept is not a licensed exchange or a financial service.
